Synthesised in the app (www/js/audiofx.js), original work with no third-party samples:
  whoosh, boom (deep bass hit), record scratch, boing, "bruh"-style womp, sad trombone,
  heartbeat, riser, typing bursts, and every background music loop (lo-fi, upbeat, chill, suspense).
  These are generated on the device when a video is rendered.

No copyrighted meme audio is included. If you add a TikTok "trending sound" in the TikTok app after uploading,
that sound is licensed by TikTok for use on TikTok only.
